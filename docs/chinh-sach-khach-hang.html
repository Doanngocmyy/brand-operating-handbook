Shipping & Delivery

Made to order, shipped by sea. Each order is produced by our partner workshop, quality checked, and shipped by sea. Typical door-to-door time:

  • Australia: 8–12 weeks
  • Singapore: 5–8 weeks

Your estimated delivery window is shown on the product page and in your order confirmation.

Real tracking only. We send your tracking number once the carrier has physically scanned your parcel. Until then, we email you every 7 days with the current stage: in production → quality checked → departed port → arrived → out for delivery.

Delivery booking. The local carrier will contact you to book a delivery day. Standard delivery is to your front door (ground floor). Two-person delivery and assembly are optional add-ons where available.

Multiple boxes. Larger items arrive in several boxes, each marked "Box X of Y". Please count them before signing.

If we are late. If your order has not been delivered by the end of your estimated window, you can choose to keep waiting with a late-delivery credit, or cancel for a full refund.

Returns, Refunds & Faulty Items

Damaged or faulty on arrival. Please send photos (the item, the box label and the damage) within 7 days so we can claim with the carrier quickly. This does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. We will repair, replace or refund. For a major failure, you choose.

Wrong item or missing parts. We send the correct item or the missing parts at our cost. Missing fittings are sent by express post within 5 business days.

Not as described. If the size, colour or material is significantly different from our listing, you are entitled to a replacement or a full refund.

Large items. If a faulty item is too large to return easily, we arrange and pay for collection.

Change of mind. Unused, unassembled items in original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. You pay the return freight. We refund within 7 business days after we receive the item.

Cancellations. Free cancellation before production starts (usually within 48 hours of ordering). After that, cancellation is possible with the fee shown at checkout, unless we are late or the item is faulty.

Refund timing. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 business days of approval.
